# Insights & Recommendations

## Insight 1 — Payment Method Performance

### Finding

Bank Transfer has the highest failure rate
at 10.78%, with 11 failed transactions
out of 102 attempts.

### Insight

Although Bank Transfer has fewer transaction
attempts than other methods, its higher
failure rate suggests an area that may
require further investigation.

### Recommendation

Analyze failure reasons and payment flows
for Bank Transfer transactions.

### Limitation

The available data does not establish
the underlying technical or operational
cause of these failures.


## Insight 2 — Failure Reason Analysis

### Finding

Insufficient Funds accounts for 24 of
62 failed transactions, representing 38.71%.

### Insight

A significant share of failed transactions
is concentrated in this error category.

This creates an opportunity to review
customer-facing error messages.

### Recommendation

Review error messaging and the next steps
provided to customers after payment failure.


## Insight 3 — Merchant Category Performance

### Finding

Travel generated 894,676.99 TRY in
successful transaction volume from
110 successful transactions.

Grocery recorded 241 successful transactions
but generated 144,695.20 TRY in volume.

### Insight

Travel stands out in monetary volume,
while Grocery stands out in transaction
frequency.

### Recommendation

Evaluate merchant category performance
using transaction count, total volume,
and average transaction amount together.
